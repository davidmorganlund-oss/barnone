Reentry help / Idaho

Idaho reentry resources

What to do first, where to get help, and the rules that apply to you in Idaho. Every link goes to the agency or organization itself.

Know the rules

Four things to know in Idaho

Voting

Your right to vote comes back automatically when you finish your whole sentence, including probation and parole. Then register again at VoteIdaho.gov.

State ID

Since 2019, the Department of Correction and the Idaho Transportation Department have issued state ID cards to people leaving some prisons. Ask your case manager before release. If you leave without one, see what documents ITD requires.

Health coverage

Idaho expanded Medicaid in 2020, so many adults with low incomes qualify. Apply as soon as you can after release. Federal rules for adult Medicaid are changing, so ask about any work or reporting rules when you apply.

Clearing a record

Idaho has no automatic record clearing. Options include dismissal after a withheld judgment, reducing some felonies to misdemeanors, and, since 2023, shielding one nonviolent misdemeanor or drug possession case from public view after five clean years.

Where to get help

Idaho resource directory

Leaving prison and supervision

  • Idaho Department of CorrectionRelease planning, community reentry centers, and probation and parole.
  • Commission of Pardons and ParoleParole hearings, pardons, and firearm rights applications.

Health care and recovery

  • Apply for Idaho MedicaidOnline at idalink, by phone at 1-877-456-1233, or at a Health and Welfare office.
  • Recovery community centersFree peer support, meetings, and a place to land, in communities across the state.
  • FindTreatment.govSearch for substance use and mental health treatment near you, including medication for opioid use disorder.

Housing, food, and basics

  • Idaho 211 CareLineDial 211 for shelter, food, utility help, and local services.
  • Apply for food assistance (SNAP)By phone at 1-877-456-1233, at a Health and Welfare office, or with a paper application.
  • Idaho Housing and Finance AssociationRental help and lists of affordable housing.

Work

  • Idaho Department of LaborJob search, training, and local offices. Idaho has no statewide fair chance hiring law for private employers, so ask reentry programs which employers hire people with records.

Legal help and your record

  • Idaho Legal Aid ServicesFree civil legal help for people with low incomes, including housing and family law.
  • Idaho Volunteer Lawyers ProgramFree help from volunteer attorneys.
  • Idaho Court Assistance OfficeCourt forms and self-help guides, including motions about your case.
  • ACLU of IdahoRights information and policy advocacy.

Last checked Nov. 11, 2026. Rules and programs change, so confirm with the agency or a lawyer before you rely on them. This page is general information, not legal advice. Know a resource we should add, or a link that's broken? Tell us.
