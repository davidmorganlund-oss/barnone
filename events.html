Idaho Reentry Summit on Criminal Justice

The Idaho Reentry Series brought stakeholders from across Idaho together to discuss reentry while the Legislature worked on criminal justice bills, including the Fair Chance Employment Act.

A BarNone tradition

The release party

Our original signature event welcomed returning citizens back into the community with dinner, motivational speakers, local resources, and prizes. Speakers included people who had come home and thrived, public officials, corrections professionals, and authors.

The idea is simple. Coming home should feel like a welcome, not a warning.
